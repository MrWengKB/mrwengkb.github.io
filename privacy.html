Privacy Policy隐私政策

MrWengKB's Creations · Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy covers the websites, games and apps published by MrWengKB at mrwengkb.github.io and on Google Play, including Class Manager and Honeycomb War. Where a project has its own privacy page, that page adds details for that project.

No server, no data sent to the developer

These projects have no server of their own. They run in your browser or on your device. The developer does not receive, store, sell or share your personal data or the content you create.

Signing in with Google

Some projects let you sign in with your Google account. Signing in is optional where the project says so (for example, Honeycomb War can be played without signing in). When you sign in, the project asks Google for:

  • Your name and email address (openid, email, profile), only to show who is signed in and to keep each person's data separate on a shared device.
  • Google Drive, limited to files the project creates (drive.file). Your data is saved as files in a folder named after the project, such as “Class Manager” or “Honeycomb War”, in your own Google Drive. The project cannot see, change or delete any other files in your Drive.

The sign-in token is kept in your browser and expires after about an hour. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the project. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone, and not read by any person.

What is stored and where

  • Class Manager: class layouts, class lists and student names, seating plans, groups, roles, points and reading logs, saved in your Google Drive and in your browser's local storage.
  • Honeycomb War: your level, win/draw/loss record, settings and the game in progress, saved as one encrypted file in your Google Drive (if signed in) and encrypted in your browser's local storage. Without signing in, it stays only in that browser.
  • Other projects keep their settings and progress in your browser's local storage on your device, unless their own page says otherwise.

Analytics

Some websites, including this homepage and Class Manager, use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It uses cookies and does not include your class data, names you enter, game data or Google Drive files. Honeycomb War does not use analytics.

Ads in Android apps

Some Android apps have an optional “Support” button. Only if you tap it, the app shows a video ad from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ad interaction, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads never receive your app data or Google Drive files. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. The websites show no ads.

Children

Some projects are made for classrooms. Student information in Class Manager is entered and controlled by the teacher, stored in the teacher's own Google Drive, and never sent to the developer. Ads, where offered, are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the project's folder (for example “Class Manager” or “Honeycomb War”) in Google Drive, then empty the Drive trash.
  • Remove the project's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

Class Manager has a step-by-step page: Delete your account and data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue on the project's repository at github.com/MrWengKB.

本政策适用于 MrWengKB 在 mrwengkb.github.io 和 Google Play 上发布的网站、游戏和应用，包括课堂管家（Class Manager）和蜂巢之战（Honeycomb War）。个别项目如有自己的隐私页面，会在那里补充该项目的细节。

没有服务器，不会把资料传给开发者

这些项目没有自己的服务器，只在你的浏览器或设备上运行。开发者不会收到、保存、出售或分享你的个人资料或你建立的内容。

用 Google 账号登录

部分项目可以用 Google 账号登录。项目注明可选时，登录是可选的（例如蜂巢之战不登录也能玩）。登录时，项目会向 Google 申请：

  • 你的名字和电邮地址（openid、email、profile），只用来显示登录的是谁，并在共用设备上把每个人的资料分开。
  • Google Drive，但仅限项目自己建立的文件（drive.file）。资料会保存在你自己 Google Drive 里以项目命名的文件夹，例如「Class Manager」或「Honeycomb War」。项目看不到、也不能修改或删除你 Drive 里的其他文件。

登录凭证保存在你的浏览器里，大约一小时后失效，只会发送给 Google。

从 Google API 取得的资料，其使用和传输遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括「有限使用」要求。这些资料只用于提供你在项目里看到的功能，不用于广告，不转交任何人，也不会有人查看。

保存什么、保存在哪里

  • 课堂管家：教室布局、班级名单和学生名字、座位安排、小组、职务、加分和阅读记录，保存在你的 Google Drive 和浏览器的本地存储里。
  • 蜂巢之战：你的等级、胜负和平局记录、设置和进行中的对局，加密后保存为你 Google Drive 里的一个文件（如已登录），并加密保存在浏览器的本地存储里。不登录时，资料只留在那个浏览器里。
  • 其他项目把设置和进度保存在你设备上浏览器的本地存储里，除非该项目的页面另有说明。

访问统计

部分网站（包括这个主页和课堂管家）使用 Google Analytics 统计访问次数和页面使用情况。这会用到 cookie，但不包含班级资料、你输入的名字、游戏资料或 Google Drive 文件。蜂巢之战不使用访问统计。

安卓应用里的广告

部分安卓应用有一个可选的「支持作者」按钮。只有你点了它，应用才会播放一段 Google AdMob 的视频广告。AdMob 可能会收集你设备的广告 ID、IP 地址和广告互动资料，详见 Google 如何使用使用其服务的网站或应用中的信息。广告永远拿不到你的应用资料或 Google Drive 文件。你可以在手机的「设置 → Google → 广告」重设或删除广告 ID。网站本身不显示广告。

儿童

部分项目是为课堂设计的。课堂管家里的学生资料由老师输入和管理，保存在老师自己的 Google Drive 里，不会传给开发者。如有广告，只限适合所有年龄的内容。

删除资料

  • 在 Google Drive 删除项目的文件夹（例如「Class Manager」或「Honeycomb War」），并清空回收站。
  • 在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 取消项目对你 Google 账号的访问权限。
  • 清除浏览器里该网站的资料，或卸载应用。

课堂管家有详细步骤：删除账号和资料。

修改与联系

本政策如有修改，新版本会以新的日期发布在本页。如有疑问，请在 github.com/MrWengKB 上对应项目的仓库提交 issue。
